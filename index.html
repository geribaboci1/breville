The Morning
Ritual.

A three-film series built around the quiet, tactile ritual of making coffee at home — created to make the Barista Touch™ Impress feel less like an appliance and more like part of the morning.

Proposed product
the Barista Touch™ Impress
The idea
Make the everyday feel cinematic.

The campaign follows one morning from first light to first sip. Close-up textures, natural sound, intentional camera movement and cinematic lighting turn a familiar routine into something viewers want to experience themselves.

01 / Campaign

FILM 02

The Ritual

FILM 03

First Sip

The payoff. Latte art, steam, the finished cup and the moment the day actually starts — ending on the feeling rather than a hard sell.

04 / Why Breville

Coffee is already cinematic.

Breville gives the ritual the precision and craft behind it. The Barista Touch™ Impress combines guided preparation, assisted dosing and tamping, precise extraction and hands-free microfoam — giving the camera a sequence of tactile moments worth filming.

3 × vertical films

Three cinematic Instagram Reels across one month, designed as a connected visual series rather than isolated product posts.

Cross-platform

Content can be cross-posted to TikTok to extend the campaign's reach and give the creative a second native distribution channel.

Product storytelling

Features are integrated naturally into the morning ritual: touchscreen guidance, grinding, the Impress Puck System, extraction and microfoam.

Premium production

Cinematic lighting, macro detail, controlled camera movement, sound design and color grading built to feel at home beside premium lifestyle advertising.
